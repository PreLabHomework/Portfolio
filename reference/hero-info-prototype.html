Debrief

TremorMonitor is a wearable sleeve and phone app for tracking Parkinson's tremor. The sleeve samples an IMU, detects tremor in the frequency domain, and drives an actuator in response. The phone logs each session and exports it for clinicians.

I was the only software developer on a four-person senior design team, so the firmware, the Bluetooth link, and the app were all mine. Most of the hard work was in the link between them: a byte-order bug in the BLE UUIDs, a write path that needed rewriting, and disconnects that had to fail cleanly instead of hanging the app.

We demoed it at the SLU symposium in April 2026, and the finished system was sold to a research lab.
